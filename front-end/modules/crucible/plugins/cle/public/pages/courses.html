Import Roster

Upload a CSV with email, first name and last name columns (an export from Canvas works as-is). Students without an account get one, and are emailed a link to choose their own password. Students who already have an account are just added to this course.

Generate Cohort Accounts

Creates numbered lab accounts named after this course's code, with random passwords — for exercises where students shouldn't need a real email address. No email is sent; you'll get the credentials once, here, to print or hand out.

Names already in use are skipped, so you can add more to an existing cohort later.
Leave this off if you're handing out a printed sheet — turning it on means the passwords below stop working once each student signs in.
